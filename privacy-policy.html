This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Arrow Mind: Puzzle Path (the “Game”, bundle ID com.lightgame.arrowmind) handles information. The Game is developed and published by an independent developer (“we”, “us”, “our”).

In short: your progress and settings stay on your device. The Game is free and is supported by advertising, so it uses third-party analytics, attribution and advertising SDKs. Those SDKs receive device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and gameplay events. On iOS we ask for App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking; if you decline, the Game still works and you will see non-personalised ads. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Game on iOS, iPadOS and Android, and to this website. It does not apply to the App Store, Google Play, or any other third-party service you may reach from the Game.

2. Information collected by the Game’s service providers

The Game integrates the following third-party SDKs. They collect the data listed below when you launch and play the Game:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android), app-scoped identifiers (IDFV / app instance ID), device model, operating-system version, language, country or approximate region derived from your IP address, and IP address.
  • Advertising data — which ads were offered, shown or clicked, the mediating ad network, and estimated ad revenue.
  • Gameplay and usage data — level started, completed or failed, session length, difficulty tier, achievements, daily streak, and settings changes.
  • Diagnostic data — crash logs, performance and stability events.

We do not collect your name, email address or phone number (unless you choose to email support), postal address, contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, health data, or payment information. The Game has no account system and no in-app purchases.

3. How the information is used

  • to show ads that keep the Game free to play, and to measure whether those ads work;
  • to attribute installs and campaigns to their source;
  • to understand which levels are too easy, too hard or broken, and to improve them;
  • to diagnose crashes and performance problems;
  • to detect invalid traffic and advertising fraud.

We do not use this information to build a profile of you for any purpose other than advertising and analytics as described here.

5. Tracking, advertising identifiers and App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, before the Game uses your advertising identifier for tracking across apps and websites, iOS shows an App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can decline; the Game remains fully playable and ads become non-personalised. You can change the choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in Settings → Google → Ads.

In the EEA and the United Kingdom, the Game uses AppLovin’s consent management flow to ask for your consent to personalised advertising before ads are requested. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the Game under Settings → Ad Privacy, which re-opens the same consent dialog, or by contacting us.

6. Legal bases (GDPR and UK GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • consent for advertising identifiers, personalised advertising and tracking (you can withdraw it at any time);
  • legitimate interests for analytics, security, fraud prevention and improving the Game;
  • performance of a contract or steps at your request when you contact support.

7. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You may also have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

  • Advertising choices: open Settings → Ad Privacy in the Game to re-open the consent dialog (EEA/UK), or use the iOS tracking setting and the Android advertising-ID controls.
  • Deletion: all progress and settings are stored only on your device; deleting the Game removes them. To ask the providers above to delete data associated with your device, contact us and we will pass the request on.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell personal information for money. Our advertising partners may “share” identifiers for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out with the tracking and advertising controls described in section 5. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

8. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following to its own private storage so that it works from one session to the next:

  • level progress, unlocked levels, stars, and best scores;
  • daily challenge results and your streak;
  • hearts (lives), hints, and achievement records;
  • your preferences (language, sound, music, vibration, hints overlay, theme);
  • whether you have accepted the in-app terms on first launch.

This data stays in the Game’s sandbox. It is deleted when you delete the Game. If you have device backups enabled, the save file may be part of that backup; the backup is controlled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

9. Children’s privacy

The Game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is not directed to children, and we do not use the Game to build children’s profiles. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it and pass the request to the providers listed in section 4.

10. Retention, security and international transfers

The providers in section 4 keep data for the periods described in their own policies (typically up to 24 months for analytics and attribution records). Support emails are kept for up to 90 days after the conversation ends, unless the law requires a longer period.

Data may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not hold a database of player data ourselves; the strongest protection is that the Game has no account and stores your progress locally.

11. Changes and contact

If the Game’s data practices change, we will update this policy, revise the “Last updated” date, and, where required, ask for your consent in the Game before the change takes effect. The current version is always the one linked from the Game’s Settings screen and from the store listing.
