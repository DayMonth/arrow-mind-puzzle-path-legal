Privacy Policy — Arrow Mind: Puzzle Path

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Arrow Mind: Puzzle Path (the “Game”, bundle ID com.lightgame.arrowmind) handles information. The Game is developed and published by an independent developer (“we”, “us”, “our”).

In short: the Game does not collect your personal data. It is a fully offline single-player puzzle game. It has no account system, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything you do in the Game is stored on your own device and stays there.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Game on iOS, iPadOS and Android, and to this website. It does not apply to the App Store, Google Play, or any other third-party service you may reach from the Game.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or postal address;
  • your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location;
  • your device identifiers, advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID), or IP address;
  • your gameplay, level progress, scores, streaks, or in-app behaviour;
  • crash logs, diagnostics, or usage statistics generated by the Game.

The Game contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no attribution SDK, and no social or login SDK. It does not request permission to track you across apps or websites, and it shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to track. The Game does not require an internet connection to play.

3. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following to its own private storage on your device so that it works from one session to the next:

  • level progress, unlocked levels, stars, and best scores;
  • daily challenge results and your streak;
  • hearts (lives), hints, and achievement records;
  • your preferences (language, sound, music, vibration, hints overlay, theme);
  • whether you have accepted the in-app terms on first launch.

This data never leaves your device. We have no ability to read, copy, or recover it. It is deleted when you delete the Game. If you have device backups enabled and your platform includes app data in them, the Game’s save file may be part of that backup; the backup itself is controlled by Apple or Google under their own policies, not by us.

4. Information you choose to send us

If you use Feedback or Contact Support in the Game, the Game opens your device’s mail app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent until you press send, and you can edit or discard it first. The draft includes the message you write plus basic app and device details (app version, device model, OS version) so that we can reproduce a problem.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you attach, such as a screenshot. We use it only to answer you and to fix the issue. Support email is kept for up to 90 days after the conversation ends and is then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

You are never required to contact us, and you may ask us to delete a support conversation at any time.

5. External links

The Game has buttons that open pages outside the app: the App Store or Google Play listing (Rate Us / View App), this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service, and your mail app. Once a link opens, the destination service’s own privacy policy applies. We do not control those services and do not receive information about what you do there.

6. Platform-level data

Apple and Google may collect information about app installs, crashes, and device diagnostics under their own privacy policies. If you have opted in to share diagnostics with developers, we may receive aggregated, non-identifying crash reports through App Store Connect or Google Play Console. We use them only to diagnose and fix defects, never for advertising or tracking, and never combine them with other information.

7. Children’s privacy

The Game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because the Game collects no personal information at all, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your privacy rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and the CCPA/CPRA give you rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because we do not collect personal data through the Game, there is normally nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete.

You can erase all Game data yourself at any time by deleting the app; on Android you can also clear the app’s storage in system settings.

For support emails we hold, you may request access, correction, or deletion by writing to the address in section 11. We respond within the period required by applicable law and will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Legal bases (GDPR). Where the GDPR applies, we process support emails on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you and, where relevant, on the consent you give by sending the message. We do not process Game data at all.

9. Security, retention, and international transfers

The Game stores data only in its own sandbox, protected by your device’s operating system and, where enabled, your device passcode and encryption.

We keep support emails only as long as described in section 4. Our email provider may store messages on servers outside your country; where required, such transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards under applicable law.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but the strongest protection here is structural: we hold no database of player data that could be breached.

10. Changes to this policy

If the Game’s data practices change — for example if a future version adds online features, advertising, or in-app purchases — we will update this policy, revise the “Last updated” date, and, where required, ask for your consent in the app before the change takes effect. The current version of this policy is always the one linked from the Game’s Settings screen and from the store listing.
