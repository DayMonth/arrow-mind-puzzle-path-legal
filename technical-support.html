This page explains how to get help with Arrow Mind: Puzzle Path (bundle ID com.lightgame.arrowmind), answers the questions we receive most often, and tells you what to include so that we can fix a problem quickly.

The fastest way to reach us is email: HaggisHackerott273@gmail.com. You can also use Feedback in the Game’s Settings screen, which opens your mail app with the technical details already filled in.

1. Supported versions

  • iOS / iPadOS: version 15.0 or later.
  • Android: version 7.0 (API 24) or later.
  • An internet connection is not required to play. All levels, including the daily challenge, work fully offline.

2. Before you write to us

A few problems have a simple fix that is worth trying first:

  • Restart the Game completely (swipe it away from the app switcher) and open it again.
  • Restart your device. This clears most memory and audio-session problems.
  • Check for updates in the App Store or Google Play — the issue may already be fixed.
  • Free up storage. The Game needs a small amount of free space to save progress.

3. Reporting a bug

Please include as much of the following as you can. A short, specific report is far more useful than a long one.

  • the app version (Settings → About, or the store listing);
  • your device model and OS version (for example “iPhone 15, iOS 18.2” or “Pixel 8, Android 15”);
  • the level number or screen where it happened;
  • what you expected to happen and what actually happened;
  • the steps that reproduce it, if you can repeat it;
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if the problem is visual.

You can paste this template into your email:

App version: / Device: / OS: / Level or screen: / What I did: / What happened: / What I expected:

4. Frequently asked questions

I lost my progress after reinstalling. Can you restore it?

The Game saves progress only on your device; there is no account, cloud save, or server copy. If you delete the app, clear its storage, or move to a new device, that progress cannot be recovered by us or by anyone else. We are sorry — this is a deliberate privacy choice, not an oversight. Reinstalling does not reset progress as long as the app itself was not deleted.

Tip: on iOS, an iCloud or computer backup taken before the change can restore the app and its data; on Android, a Google backup can do the same if backup was enabled.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings inside the Game and choose a language. The Game supports English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean, and it does not depend on your device language unless you leave the setting on automatic.

There is no sound, or music keeps stopping.

Check the in-game sound and music toggles first, then your device’s silent switch, volume, and Focus / Do Not Disturb mode. On iOS, audio can also be interrupted by another app holding the audio session; closing that app or restarting the Game usually restores it.

How do hearts (lives) work? Do I have to pay?

Each level starts with 3 hearts, and every mistake costs one. Starting the level again, or finishing it, refills them. If you run out, the Game offers a free revive that refills all 3 hearts so you can continue the same board — no video, no waiting, no purchase. The Game has no in-app purchases, so nothing in it costs money.

The daily challenge did not appear, or shows the wrong day.

The daily challenge is generated from your device’s local date, so it follows your time zone. Changing the time zone or the device clock can make today’s challenge look different or already completed. Set the clock back to automatic and reopen the Game.

The screen is stretched, cut off, or the buttons are too small.

The Game adapts to most aspect ratios, including notched and foldable devices. If something looks wrong, send us a screenshot with your device model — that is usually enough for us to fix the layout in the next update.

The Game is slow, hot, or drains the battery.

Close other apps, disable low-power mode temporarily, and make sure the Game is updated. Puzzle levels are lightweight, but a long session on a device with many background apps can still warm it up.

Can I play offline? Does it need permissions?

Yes, the whole Game works offline. It requests no sensitive permissions: no location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or tracking.
